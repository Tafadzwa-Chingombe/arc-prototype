You are not alone.Free, confidential care for survivors of sexual violence.

Women and men can come to ARC for medical care, counselling and support. You will be treated with respect, and you decide what happens.

  • Get help now
  • Find a clinic

Come as soon as you can: the 72-hour window

Come to a clinic as soon as possible, ideally within 72 hours (3 days). Early care can reduce the risk of HIV, sexually transmitted infections and unwanted pregnancy, and helps preserve evidence if you decide to report.

Even if it has been longer than 72 hours, or it happened a long time ago, you can still come for care and counselling.

  • Call 08080472
  • Read more about the 72-hour window

What happens when you come to ARC

Everything is free and confidential. You can bring someone you trust, and you can stop at any time.

  1. Arrive

    A member of staff welcomes you and takes you somewhere private.

  2. Private consultation

    A nurse or counsellor listens and explains your options. You choose what happens next.

  3. Medical care

    Examination, treatment, PEP and emergency contraception if you need them.

  4. Ongoing support

    Follow-up visits, counselling, and referrals to police or legal help if you want them.

Our services

All services are free.

  • Medical examination and treatment

    A private, gentle examination by a trained nurse or doctor, and treatment for any injuries. You decide what happens at each step.

    About this service: Medical examination and treatment

  • PEP to reduce the risk of HIV

    Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) is medicine that lowers the chance of HIV infection. It works best when started as soon as possible, within 72 hours.

    About this service: PEP to reduce the risk of HIV

  • Emergency contraception

    Medicine to help prevent an unwanted pregnancy. It works best the sooner it is taken.

    About this service: Emergency contraception

  • Evidence collection and medical affidavits

    If you choose, we can collect forensic evidence and complete the medical affidavit needed for a court case.

    About this service: Evidence collection and medical affidavits

  • Counselling and psychotherapy

    Someone to talk to, now and later. We offer short-term and long-term counselling and psychotherapy.

    About this service: Counselling and psychotherapy

  • Referrals to police and legal support

    If and when you want to report, we can support you and refer you to the Victim Friendly Unit and legal help.

    About this service: Referrals to police and legal support

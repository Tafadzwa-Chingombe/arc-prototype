Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people using phones on slow connections and people using assistive technology.

Our target

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

Accessibility features

  • Emergency help is in the same place at the top of every page.
  • A "Skip to main content" link and clear headings and landmarks.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
  • Text can be resized to 200% and the layout reflows on small screens without sideways scrolling.
  • Colour contrast meets at least 4.5:1 for text. Colour is never the only way information is shown.
  • Reduced motion is respected, and dark mode follows your device setting.
  • Documents have an HTML summary you can read on the page.
  • Quick exit works with a single tap or by pressing Esc twice.

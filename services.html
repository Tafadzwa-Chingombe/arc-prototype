Our services

Every service is free and confidential, for women and men.

  • Free

    Medical examination and treatment

    A private, gentle examination by a trained nurse or doctor, and treatment for any injuries. You decide what happens at each step.

  • Free

    PEP to reduce the risk of HIV

    Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) is medicine that lowers the chance of HIV infection. It works best when started as soon as possible, within 72 hours.

  • Free

    Emergency contraception

    Medicine to help prevent an unwanted pregnancy. It works best the sooner it is taken.

  • Free

    STI testing and treatment

    Confidential testing and treatment for sexually transmitted infections, with follow-up visits if needed.

  • Free

    HIV testing and counselling

    Free, confidential HIV testing with a counsellor who will explain each result and what it means for you.

  • Free

    Evidence collection and medical affidavits

    If you choose, we can collect forensic evidence and complete the medical affidavit needed for a court case.

  • Free

    Counselling and psychotherapy

    Someone to talk to, now and later. We offer short-term and long-term counselling and psychotherapy.

  • Free

    Referrals to police and legal support

    If and when you want to report, we can support you and refer you to the Victim Friendly Unit and legal help.

  • Free

    Training and awareness programmes

    Training for police officers, health workers and community leaders, and awareness sessions in schools, churches and communities.

PEP and emergency contraception work best when started as soon as possible. Come within 72 hours if you can, but you can still come for care and counselling at any time.

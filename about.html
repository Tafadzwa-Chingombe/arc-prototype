About ARC

Free, confidential, holistic care for survivors of sexual violence since 2009.

Our history

The Adult Rape Clinic was established in 2009 with support from the Ministry of Health and Child Care and Parirenyatwa Hospital. It was created in response to the lack of a holistic, sensitive response for rape survivors in government hospitals.

Today ARC provides free, confidential medical care and psychosocial support to women and men who have experienced sexual violence, and supports more than 40 new survivors every month.

Our vision

[Confirm with ARC] A Zimbabwe where every survivor of sexual violence receives timely, dignified and free care.

Our mission

[Confirm with ARC] To provide holistic medical and psychosocial care to survivors, and to strengthen the response of police, health workers and communities.

What we do

  • Clinic

    Free, confidential medical care and counselling for survivors, at five clinics across Zimbabwe.

  • Training

    We train police officers in the Victim Friendly Unit, health workers and other stakeholders in rape response.

  • Outreach and awareness

    Community outreach and awareness on sexual and gender-based violence in schools, churches and communities.

Summaries

Know your rights after sexual assault

You have the right to free medical care, to be treated with respect, to privacy, and to decide whether to report to the police. You can bring someone you trust with you.

Annual Report [Year]

A summary of ARC's work during the year: survivors supported, training delivered, outreach activities and finances. [Figures to be confirmed with ARC.]

What happens at the clinic

A step-by-step guide: you arrive and are welcomed, you speak privately with a nurse or counsellor, you receive medical care, and you are offered ongoing support.

Supporting a friend or family member

Listen without judging, believe them, let them make their own choices, and help them reach care quickly. Look after yourself too.

Understanding PEP and the 72-hour window

PEP is medicine that lowers the risk of HIV after exposure. It must be started within 72 hours, and the sooner the better. It is free at ARC.

16 Days of Activism awareness pack

Posters, talking points and activity ideas for schools, churches and community groups taking part in the 16 Days of Activism.

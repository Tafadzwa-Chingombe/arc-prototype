Browsing safely

If you are worried someone may check your phone or computer, these steps can help.

Using Quick exit

Every page has a Quick exit button. On phones it is at the bottom right of the screen; on larger screens it is at the top right.

  • Tap or click Quick exit to leave immediately. This page is replaced with a weather search, so pressing Back will not return to this site.
  • Keyboard shortcut: press the Esc key twice quickly (within one second).

Quick exit does not delete your history. Other pages you visited on this site may still appear in your browser history. Follow the steps below to remove them.

Use private or incognito mode

Private browsing does not save the pages you visit once you close the window.

  • Chrome: menu (three dots) → New Incognito tab.
  • Safari (iPhone): tap the tabs button → Private.
  • Samsung Internet: tap the tabs button → Turn on Secret mode.
  • Firefox: menu → New private tab (or the mask icon).

Clear your browsing history

Chrome (Android and computer)

  1. Open the menu (three dots) and choose History.
  2. Choose Delete browsing data (or remove single pages with the X next to them).
  3. Choose the time range, then Delete data.

Safari (iPhone)

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari.
  2. Choose Clear History and Website Data.
  3. Or in Safari, tap the book icon → History, and swipe left on single pages to delete them.

Samsung Internet

  1. Tap the menu (three lines) → History.
  2. Tap More (three dots) → Clear history, or long-press a single page to delete it.

Firefox

  1. Open the menu → History.
  2. Choose Clear recent history or delete single pages.

Menu names change between versions. If a step looks different, look for "History" in your browser's menu.

Use a trusted device

If possible, use a phone or computer that only you use, or one at a trusted friend's home, a library or an internet café. Remember to log out of any accounts and close the browser when you finish.

Calls to the toll-free line may still appear in your phone's call log. You can delete the entry from your call history afterwards.

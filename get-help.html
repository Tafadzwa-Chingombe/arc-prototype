After a sexual assault: what you can do

  1. Go somewhere safe. This could be a friend's or family member's home, or anywhere you feel safe.
  2. Come to ARC as soon as possible. You can also call us first on 08080472.
  3. If you can, try not to bathe, wash or change your clothes before the examination. This helps preserve evidence. But please come anyway, even if you have.
  4. Bring someone you trust if that would help.

The 72-hour window

Come to a clinic as soon as possible, ideally within 72 hours (3 days). Early care can reduce the risk of HIV, sexually transmitted infections and unwanted pregnancy, and helps preserve evidence if you decide to report.

Even if it has been longer than 72 hours, or it happened a long time ago, you can still come for care and counselling.

What to expect at the clinic

  • Confidential. Your visit is private.
  • Free. You will not pay for any service.
  • Your choice. Staff explain each step and ask before they do anything.
  • You can bring someone with you.
  • You can stop at any time.

Reporting to the police

Reporting is your choice. You can receive medical care without reporting. If you want to report, ARC can support you and refer you to the Victim Friendly Unit (VFU) of the Zimbabwe Republic Police, whose officers are trained to support survivors.

If you decide to go to court, ARC can complete the medical affidavit.

Supporting someone you care about

  • Listen, and believe them.
  • Tell them it was not their fault.
  • Let them make their own decisions.
  • Help them get to a clinic quickly, ideally within 72 hours.
  • Look after yourself too. You can call us for advice.

Common questions

Do I have to pay?

No. All ARC services are free.

Will anyone find out I came?

Your visit and your records are confidential. We will not share your information without your permission, except where the law requires it, and we will explain this to you.

Do I have to report to the police to get care?

No. You can get medical care and counselling without reporting. If you decide to report later, we can still help.

Can men come to ARC?

Yes. ARC supports women and men who have experienced sexual violence.

I showered or changed my clothes. Can I still come?

Yes, please come anyway. You can still receive care, and some evidence may still be collected.

It happened a long time ago. Is it too late?

No. You can come for care and counselling at any time, even years later.

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. You can bring a friend, family member or anyone you trust.
